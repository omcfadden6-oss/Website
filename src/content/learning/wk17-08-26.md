## My main takeaway from the week
To be honest, this week has totally fried my brain - I spent so long trying to understand the delta spikes at turns 5 and 6. I do feel like this really deepened my understanding of the physics of racing - before this, I struggled with understanding how there can be a delta change if the speeds are relatively the same, but I’ve realised that it is accumulative - if one driver has perhaps a slightly lower speed but a greater acceleration rate, this can gain them time. I feel like my view of how the delta changes isn’t as one-dimensional - differences in speed are not the only factor - plotting an acceleration graph really helped my understanding of that. It sounds like such basic physics, but seeing it in action has allowed me to truly understand it in practice.
